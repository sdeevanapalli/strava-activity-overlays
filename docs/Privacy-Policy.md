# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

ActivityOverlays ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you connect your Strava account to our application.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you authorize ActivityOverlays with Strava, we may access and process:
- Athlete profile data (for example: name, profile image, athlete ID)
- Activity data (for example: activity name, type, distance, time, elevation, route/stream data)
- OAuth tokens required to access your Strava data (access token and refresh token)
- Basic device, browser, and log data used to secure and operate the service

We do not request more data than needed to provide the core app feature.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
We use Strava data only to provide ActivityOverlays core functionality, including route visualization and analytics.

Specifically, we use this data to:
- Authenticate you through Strava OAuth
- Fetch and display your activity information inside the app
- Generate visual overlays and activity insights you request
- Maintain your authenticated session and refresh access securely

## 3. Data Sharing and Sale
We do not sell your personal data or Strava data to third parties.

We may use service providers strictly for hosting, infrastructure, logging, or security operations under contractual confidentiality obligations.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
- Tokens are stored securely on server-side infrastructure.
- We use technical and organizational safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access.
- Access to production systems is restricted to authorized operators.
- We do not expose refresh tokens to the browser.
- Production traffic should use HTTPS and secure cookies.

## 5. Data Retention
We retain Strava-linked account data only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

When you disconnect your Strava account, we deauthorize and remove stored OAuth credentials and related local session state we control.

## 6. Deauthorization and Revoking Access
You can revoke ActivityOverlays access at any time by:
- Using the in-app "Disconnect Strava" control, or
- Revoking access from your Strava account settings

When disconnected, we call Strava deauthorization and remove stored tokens from our database.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data.

## 8. Children's Privacy
ActivityOverlays is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
